### Game Details & Guide
Game Overview



Idle Shark is a browser-based idle game where players control a shark that must collect resources, build structures, and upgrade its abilities to survive in a dynamic world. The objective of the game is to gather resources, manage energy levels, and construct buildings to maximize your shark's potential.



How to Play



To play Idle Shark, follow these steps:
• Click on the "Start" button to begin a new day.
• The sun will rise, and resources will spawn at specific locations throughout the map.
• Collect resources by clicking on them or using special abilities like fishing nets.
• Use your resources to build structures such as homes, schools, and hospitals.
• Upgrade your shark's abilities through energy upgrades and research points.
• Manage your shark's hunger, thirst, and energy levels.
• The game will automatically reset the day.



Tips and Tricks for Success



Here are some tips to help you succeed in Idle Shark:
• Upgrade your shark's abilities regularly to access new resources and features.
• Build structures strategically to maximize resource collection and energy production.
• Research new technologies to unlock special items and upgrades.
• Manage your resources efficiently by prioritizing urgent tasks.
• Experiment with different building designs to optimize resource collection.



Why You Should Play It



Idle Shark offers a unique blend of strategy, management, and excitement that makes it an entertaining and engaging experience. Here are some reasons why you should play Idle Shark:
• Relaxing gameplay: Idle Shark's repetitive nature can be calming and soothing to play.
• Endless possibilities: The game's dynamic world and random events ensure that every playthrough is unique.
• Skill-building: As you progress, your shark will improve its abilities, making the game more challenging and rewarding.
• Fun graphics and sound effects: The game's colorful visuals and catchy music add to its overall charm.



Game Features



Idle Shark features include:
• A dynamic world with changing resources and events
• Special abilities like fishing nets, sharks' teeth, and fishing rods
• Energy management and hunger/thirst tracking
• Building structures such as homes, schools, hospitals, and more
• Research points for upgrading shark's abilities
