### Game Details & Guide
Game Overview



Idle Shark is a browser-based idle game where players control a shark that must collect resources, build structures, and upgrade its abilities to survive in a dynamic world. The objective of the game is to gather resources, manage energy levels, and construct buildings to maximize your shark's potential.



How to Play



To play Idle Shark, follow these steps:
• Click on the "Start" button to begin a new day.
• The sun will rise, and resources will spawn at specific locations throughout the map.
• Collect resources by clicking on them or using special abilities like fishing nets.
• Use your resources to build structures such as homes, schools, and hospitals.
• Upgrade your shark's abilities through energy upgrades and research points.
• Manage your shark's hunger, thirst, and energy levels.
• The game will automatically reset the day.



Tips and Tricks for Success



Here are some tips to help you succeed in Idle Shark:
• Upgrade your shark's abilities regularly to access new resources and features.

• Research new technologies to unlock special items and upgrades.

• Experiment with different building designs to optimize resource collection.
